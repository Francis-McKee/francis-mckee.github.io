Abram and Lot

<br>

## Abram and Lot Separate
Genesis 13

Problems:
* weird phrase about the Canaanites and the Perizzites (7b), also appears in 12:6
* Abram calls Lot his brother (8)

This story is linked to the previous story in [Genesis 12b]({{ '/blog/genesis/12b' | relative_url }}). Has Abram learned anything?

Abram let go of his plan for how God's promise would be fulfilled, as he lets Lot go. Also, Abram is the patriarch but he lets Lot pick the land he wanted to settle in first. He does not even look up until God tells him to (14).

After he showed God his character by letting Lot have the first pick, God made a promise revealing His blessing for Abram:

> Genesis 13:14-17 (NASB) - "The Lord said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, 'Now raise your eyes and look from the place where you are, northward and southward, and eastward and westward; for all the land which you see I will give to you and to your descendants forever. I will make your descendants as plentiful as the dust of the earth, so that if anyone can count the dust of the earth, then your descendants could also be counted. Arise, walk about in the land through its length and width; for I will give it to you.'"

## Abram Rescues Lot
Genesis 14

Lot pitches his tent near Sodom (13:12), and later the kings in that area go to war, and Lot is captured:

> Genesis 14:11-12 (NASB) - "Then they [the kings] took all the possessions of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food supply, and departed. They also took Lot, Abram’s nephew, and his possessions and departed, for he was living in Sodom."

Abram went to rescue Lot (with 318 soldiers), and after defeating the kings, he gives all the goods and people back, including Lot:

> Genesis 14:24 (NASB) - "I [Abram] will take nothing except what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; let them take their share."

We see Lot next in [Genesis 19]({{ '/blog/genesis/18-19' | relative_url }}).

Abram made a mistake in Egypt, learns a lesson, but does not let the mistake define him, as he again shows his character and trusts the story.

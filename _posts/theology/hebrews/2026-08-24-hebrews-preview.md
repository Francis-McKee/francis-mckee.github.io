Hebrews (Preview)

<br>

**Date:** 60-95 AD  
**Author:** Unknown (Hellenistic Jewish Christian)  
**Audience:** Jewish Christians  

Main Sections of Hebrews:
1. *Inclusio* - The Superiority of Jesus (1 - 10:18)
2. Conclusion - Practical Living (10:19 - 13:25)

The book of Hebrews contains the use of an ancient Greek literary tool called an *inclusio*. An inclusio is a bracketing tool used to identify an argument within a piece of literature. It functions similarly to a chiasm, but it does not have to have any inverted parallelism. There is no center to an inclusio, unless it also happens to be a chaism (all chiasmus structures contain an inclusio-style outer frame, but not all inclusio structures are a chiasm with inverted parallelism). You need to understand the argument made within the inclusio first, in order to then fully understand what is written outside of the inclusio.

The argument of the inclusio is that Jesus gives us a better way to live, because He knows what it is like to suffer, and He showed us the right way to deal with suffering. Therefore, we can follow Jesus and learn how to suffer and persevere well. The writer of Hebrews shows us this in the conclusion of the book, starting from the end of Hebrews 10 to the end of the book.

With the destruction of the Second Temple in Jerusalem by the Romans in 70 AD, the book of Hebrews answers an important question: What does Judaism look like without the Temple?

The rabbis believed that Judaism would continue through individual obedience to the Torah: with prayer and good deeds replacing Temple sacrifices. The Jewish author of Hebrews is making the same case using Jesus: in Christ, we also no longer have a need for sacrifice in the physical Temple.

It's not that Jesus came and did something new, but Jesus is a better version of everything Jews already hold dear in the Levitical system. Time and time again, the Jewish author of Hebrews keeps saying that what the Jews experienced in the Levitical system was a wonderful thing, but now that the Temple has been destroyed, the Jews need not fret, because Jesus has given them something even better.

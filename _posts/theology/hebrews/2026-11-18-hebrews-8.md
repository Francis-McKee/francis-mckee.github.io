A More Excellent Ministry

<br>

> Hebrews 8:1-2 (NASB) - "Now the main point in what has been said is this: we have such a high priest, who has taken His seat at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens, a minister in the sanctuary and in the true tabernacle, which the Lord set up, not man."

The writer of Hebrews brings together the main point of the previous chapter, [Hebrews 7]({{ '/blog/hebrews/7' | relative_url }}). We have a High Priest, Jesus Christ, who serves us from a position of authority in heaven. Additionally, Jesus is seated in heaven, in contrast to the continual service of the Aaronite priests, who do not have a place to sit because their work is never finished. The work of Jesus is finished, therefore He is seated in heaven.

Jesus doesn't serve as a priest in an earthly tabernacle or temple. He serves in the "true tabernacle" which the Lord erected, the original tabernacle made by God. The tabernacle of Moses was a copy of this original, and it was made by man (see [Exodus 25]({{ '/blog/exodus/25-27' | relative_url }})).

> Hebrews 8:3 (NASB) - "For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices; so it is necessary that this high priest also have something to offer."

Sacrifice for sin is essential to the concept of priesthood. Jesus represented a superior priesthood and offered a superior sacrifice. Though Jesus never offered a sacrifice according to the Law, He did offer a better sacrifice - He laid down His own life to atone for sin.

> Hebrews 8:4-5 (NASB) - "Now if He were on earth, He would not be a priest at all, since there are those who offer the gifts according to the Law; who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, just as Moses was warned by God when he was about to erect the tabernacle; for, 'See,' He says, 'that you make all things by the pattern which was shown to you on the mountain.'"

There were plenty of priests who could serve in the "copy and shadow" on earth. Yet Jesus is the only One qualified to serve in the superior heavenly priesthood. The earthly service was really only a copy and shadow of the superior heavenly service.

> Exodus 25:40 (NASB) - "See that you make them by the pattern for them, which was shown to you on the mountain."

This verse tells us that Moses' tabernacle built on earth was made according to a pattern that exists in heaven, and that was shown to Moses on the mountain. Therefore, there is a heavenly temple that served as a pattern for the earthly tabernacle and temple. Jesus' ministry as our High Priest takes place in this heavenly temple, not in the copy and shadow built on earth.

> Hebrews 8:6 (NASB) - "But now He has obtained a more excellent ministry, to the extent that He is also the mediator of a better covenant, which has been enacted on better promises."

Moses was the mediator of the Old Covenant because he brought the Israelites into covenantal relationship with God, a covenant of marriage between God and His people (see [Exodus 19]({{ '/blog/exodus/19' | relative_url }})). This Old Covenant mandated the people's obedience and continuous atonement for sin. Jesus is the Mediator of the New Covenant, a better covenant, bringing us close to God the Father by His death on the cross, once for all time. This New Covenant was established on better promises - undeserved grace and mercy by faith in Jesus, despite our varying levels of obedience.

The fact that God mentions another covenant proves there is something lacking in the Old Covenant.

> Hebrews 8:7 (NASB) - "For if that first covenant had been free of fault, no circumstances would have been sought for a second."

For in view of the sin of the people under the Old Covenant, God said:

> Jeremiah 31:31-34 (NASB) - "'Behold, days are coming,' declares the Lord, 'when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and the house of Judah, not like the covenant which I made with their fathers on the day I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, My covenant which they broke, although I was a husband to them,' declares the Lord. 'For this is the covenant which I will make with the house of Israel after those days,' declares the Lord: 'I will put My law within them and write it on their heart; and I will be their God, and they shall be My people. They will not teach again, each one his neighbor and each one his brother, saying, "Know the Lord," for they will all know Me, from the least of them to the greatest of them,' declares the Lord, 'for I will forgive their wrongdoing, and their sin I will no longer remember.'"

The message was clear to these discouraged Christians from Jewish backgrounds, who thought of going back to a more Jewish faith. They simply can't go back to an inferior covenant, which was ready to completely vanish away. In fact, the system of sacrifice according to the Law did vanish away with the destruction of the Temple by the Romans in 70 AD.

> Hebrews 8:13 (NASB) - "When He said, 'A new covenant,' He has made the first obsolete. But whatever is becoming obsolete and growing old is about to disappear."

Reminder: the fact that this Old Covenant is "obselete" does not mean that the Law is should no longer be considered as important, it simply lacks the power to transform your life without faith in Jesus and His work for you on the cross:

> Romans 7:12-13 (NASB) - "So then, the Law is holy, and the commandment is holy and righteous and good. Therefore did that which is good become a cause of death for me? Far from it! Rather it was sin, in order that it might be shown to be sin by bringing about my death through that which is good, so that through the commandment sin would become utterly sinful."
